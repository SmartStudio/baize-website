# chatgpt-contract-paste-vs-redact illustrations

## Shared visual direction

Clean Swiss editorial 3D material illustration, off-white studio backdrop, black ink outlines, refined gray surfaces, site orange `#E9961A` only, calm analytical light. No real logos, watermarks, ChatGPT wordmark, UI chrome, decorative gradients, or cute characters.

Cover is wordless. Body figure uses short Simplified Chinese labels on large white callout plates.

## Cover (wordless)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 16:9 note cover (≥1200×675). A single main visual: a physical contract folder / legal document stack in the left-center, with solid orange redaction bars covering stamps, names and amounts; a clean stripped document slab enters a 3D model cube / gateway on the right — NOT a chat bubble, NOT a messaging app window. The picture says: originals are masked before they reach the model. No text of any kind. Full subject visible, generous margins.

Target: `site/src/content/notes/media/chatgpt-contract-paste-vs-redact-cover.png`

## Body figure (before/after)

Use case: infographic-diagram. Asset type: wide 16:9 or 1.9:1 note figure. Before/after transformation.

- Left (before): a messy original contract with seals/stamps and visible amounts flowing into a consumer endpoint — a laptop plus a cloud/model cube. Do NOT depict ChatGPT as only a chat box.
- Center: orange redaction bars / a masking gate removing names, amounts, seals.
- Right (after): a cleaned document entering an enterprise model/gateway cube, which outputs a small risk-list card.

Chinese labels, exactly these four, on large white printed callout plates, horizontal, high-contrast, away from edges, no other text:

1. `原件进聊天框` (left)
2. `拿掉印章` (center)
3. `脱敏后再交模型` (right path)
4. `疑点清单` (output card)

Target: `site/src/content/notes/media/chatgpt-contract-paste-vs-redact-fig.png`
